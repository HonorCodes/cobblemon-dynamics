This sheet lists what Cobblemon Dynamics 1.0 does. 1.0 is the first public release. It ships in
three phases (see the Milestones table and the REL rows). Phase 1 builds and tests the v1 scope
privately. Phase 2 adds the v2 roles, Quick Work and the final art, and is also tested privately.
Phase 3 adds the land claim checks of pastures and MineColonies, and then publishes. Rows in the TLK,
MAG, FAN, TLW, QWK and ART areas belong to Phase 2, and so do CD-CFG-16 to CD-CFG-19, CD-CMP-08 to
CD-CMP-10, CD-CLM-10, CD-CLM-11, CD-LSH-28 and CD-LSH-29. Rows in the LND area belong to Phase 3, and so do
CD-QWK-36 and CD-MUS-07. All other rows belong to Phase 1, and the BAL rows apply to the roles of each phase.

Version 1.1 follows the public release 1.0.0. It adds two farming roles, the Cultivator and the Harvester, and the crop
adapters that let them work the crops of other mods (R72, R73, milestone M10). Rows in the CUL, HRV and CRP areas belong
to 1.1, and so do CD-BAL-08, CD-BAL-09 and CD-REL-16. The coordinator agreed the rows of 1.1 on 2026-11-12 (R74), and the approver agreed the last six,
CD-CUL-04, CD-CUL-06, CD-CUL-08, CD-CUL-10, CD-CRP-01 and CD-BAL-08, the same day with the answers to Q10 and Q11
(R75).

Each row states one behaviour and holds the test that proves it. A phase is done when every MUST
and MUST NOT row in its scope has a passing test. Nobody builds against a `draft` row. The approver
moves rows to `agreed`.

Design context lives in `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/roles.md`, the ADRs in `docs/adr/` and the
research notes in `docs/research/2026-11-09-integration-points.md`.

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Work Mode | A per-pasture setting. While it is on, Dynamics controls the owned Cobblemon that are tethered to that pasture. |
| work area | The box around a pasture block in which its workers find targets and beds. By default it reaches 8 blocks horizontally and 6 blocks vertically from the pasture block in each direction. |
| worker | A Cobblemon tethered to a pasture in Work Mode. A healer is a worker too. |
| target | The block or entity that a worker acts on: a machine, a crop, a bed or a healed ally. |
| canonical target | The one position that stands for a multiblock target, for example the controller of a large water wheel. |
| claim | A claim registry entry that gives one worker one target for now. The kinds are the work claim (on a machine or a container), the bed claim (on a bed) and the heal claim (on an ally). The work engine makes every claim. Claims are never saved. Every claim is an exclusive claim, except the work claim of a logistics role, which is a shared claim (CD-CLM-10). |
| leash binding | A reservation that a player makes with the Work Leash. It is not a claim. It is saved with the Cobblemon, and it reserves its target for that worker, also while the worker sleeps or its pasture's Work Mode is off. A binding of a logistics role reserves no target. It takes a place at each container that it names (CD-LSH-29). |
| logistics role | A role whose handler moves items into or out of containers and shares its targets: the Magnet and Telekinesis. A role file that names such a handler is a logistics role too. Every role that is neither a logistics role nor a farming role is an exclusive role, and its claims and bindings are exclusive. |
| farming role | The Cultivator or the Harvester (1.1). On a crop, the claims and bindings of one farming role never block those of the other farming role (CD-CRP-06, CD-CRP-07). A role file that names the handler of either is a farming role too. |
| crop | A block of the tag `cobblemon_dynamics:cultivator_targets` or `cobblemon_dynamics:harvester_targets` that a crop adapter reads for that role. A crop of two blocks, such as hemp, is one crop at its lower block. |
| crop adapter | The code that reads one family of crops for the farming roles: whether a crop can still grow, whether it is fully grown, and what a harvest drops and leaves. The adapter table of ADR-0015 lists the adapters and the crop families that each one reads. |
| fully grown | The state in which a crop's adapter lets the Harvester harvest it, for example the maximum age of a `CropBlock`. A Cultivator stops there too. |
| growth use | One application of the bonemeal effect to a crop by a Cultivator. It uses no bone meal item and costs Work Stamina (CD-CUL-06). |
| replant item | A dropped item that places a crop's own block, a `BlockItem` whose `getBlock()` is that block, such as wheat seeds for wheat, a carrot for carrots or a Pam's crop item for its crop. |
| place | One of the `logistics.maxWorkersPerContainer` places of a container at its canonical position. Each binding of a logistics role takes one place at each container that it names: the Magnet's container, or the Telekinesis source and destination. |
| auto-assigned worker | A worker with no leash binding. The work engine picks its targets. |
| Quick Work order | A one-shot work order of a party Cobblemon. It starts when the owner sends the Cobblemon out while the crosshair is on a block that one of its auto-assign roles works. It holds one work claim, never searches for other work, and ends for good. The QWK rows hold its rules. |
| Quick Worker | A party Cobblemon on a Quick Work order. No pasture holds it, so a row about workers applies to it only where a QWK row says so. |
| aimed block | The block that the owner's view hit when the owner sent out a Quick Worker. The leash range of the order counts from it. |
| claim mod | FTB Chunks, Open Parties and Claims or MineColonies. Each protects the chunks that players or colonies claim with it, and decides who can use the blocks there. |
| land claim | A chunk that a claim mod protects, such as a chunk of a MineColonies colony. It is not a claim. |
| colony | A MineColonies colony. It claims the chunks around its center, and the ranks of its players decide who can use the blocks there. |
| pasture check | The question that the work engine asks each claim mod for a Work Mode pasture: may the pasture's placer, online or not, use a block with an empty main hand. The LND rows hold its rules. |
| break question | The question that the Harvester asks each claim mod before a harvest that breaks a block: may the pasture's placer, by UUID, or the online owner of a Quick Worker break that block. The rows CD-HRV-16 to CD-HRV-21 hold its rules. |
| binder | The owner of a worker that has a leash binding. |
| pending source | The first container of a Telekinesis binding, which the Work Leash holds while it waits for the destination click. |
| role | A job that a datapack role file defines: eligible types, an auto-assign flag, a priority, a range, a handler, handler parameters and an effect. |
| role file | The datapack JSON file of one role. It holds the role-specific numbers: rates, formula constants, level gates, ranges, effect ids and the priority. It holds no species lists. |
| server config | The NeoForge server config `cobblemon_dynamics-server.toml`. NeoForge 21.1 writes it to the instance's `config/` folder. A copy in a world's `serverconfig/` folder overrides it for that world. It holds the engine-wide numbers: work area, scan cadence and budget, stamina per level, bed factors, healer recovery and timeouts. |
| handler | The code that finds, checks and works one family of targets. |
| demand | The target can use work now, for example a furnace whose recipe can run. |
| driven target | A wheel, a hand crank or a fan that a worker is Working on. |
| Idle, Traveling, Working, Standby, ToBed, Sleeping, RestingInPlace, Healing | The worker states. A worker is in exactly one of them. Working means at the work spot with demand. Standby means the worker waits with no work: at the work spot with no demand, or, for a leashed worker, during the wait after a missed arrival. ToBed means walking to a claimed bed. Sleeping means lying on a bed. RestingInPlace means sleeping where the worker stands. Healing means a healer walks to an ally or heals it. |
| Tired | A flag, not a state. The stamina component sets it when Work Stamina reaches 0% and clears it at 100%. A Tired worker is in ToBed, Sleeping or RestingInPlace. |
| worker rule | Full recovery from 0% to 100% takes 3 min × level. |
| healer rule | Full recovery takes 4 × (300 − 270 × (L − 1) ÷ 99) min. It applies to a Cobblemon with a Fairy type and no machine binding. |
| work focus entity | An invisible, unsaved entity at a target's center. Only a Working worker has one. Cobblemon particles aim at it. |
| placer | The player that Cobblemon records in the pasture block entity's `ownerId` when the pasture is placed. `ownerId` is the one source of pasture ownership. |
| operator | A player with permission level 2 or higher. |
| legendary or mythical | A Cobblemon whose form carries the Cobblemon label `legendary` or `mythical`. |
| Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3 | The release phases: the private test of v1, the private test of v2, and the public release. The Milestones table lists their milestones. |
| 1.1 | The first version after the public release 1.0.0. It adds the farming roles (milestone M10). |

## Goals

- G1: A player switches a Cobblemon pasture to Work Mode, and its owned Cobblemon power and operate machines in the work area, with no stand block.
- G2: Water-types turn Create water wheels with no water, Electric-types generate Forge Energy by level, and Fire-types fuel furnaces as if they burned coal.
- G3: Thirteen types get fifteen roles across vanilla Minecraft, Create and Applied Energistics 2: eleven in Phase 1 and four in Phase 2.
- G4: Only one worker acts on a target at a time, across all pastures and players, and wild Cobblemon never work.
- G5: A player binds a specific pastured Cobblemon to a specific machine with the Work Leash.
- G6: Work Stamina, four bed tiers and Fairy healers make worker uptime something that a player plans, as in Palworld.
- G7: Players see the state and Work Stamina of each worker in the world and in the pasture panel.
- G8: Dynamics runs in one modpack with Cobbleworkers and Cobblebase.
- G9: 1.0 ships as a public MPL-2.0 release on Modrinth and CurseForge after two private test phases, and joins the approver's modpack.
- G10: No single Cobblemon solves an engineering problem that the player is meant to manage. Cobblemon are an early-to-midgame option that a fleet can make competitive, and top-tier engineering always outperforms them.
- G11: Every new block, item and UI sprite uses original art in vanilla 16 × 16 style, and the approver approves each piece before the public release.
- G12: In 1.1, Ground-types grow crops and Grass-types harvest and replant them, for vanilla crops and the crop mods of the COHO modpack, with a leash for one crop or none for a whole field.

## Non-goals

- A Cobblemon Work Stand block. Work Mode on the pasture replaces it (ADR 0001).
- Fabric, Forge, and Minecraft versions other than 1.21.1 (ADR 0002).
- Jobs that make items from nothing. Cobbleworkers, Cobblebase and Cobblemon Pasture Loot cover loot.
- The later ideas: Pollinator (Bug) and Freezer (Ice). `docs/roles.md` holds them. In 1.1, the Harvester (R72) takes the
  place of the Grower (Grass) idea.
- A role for the Dark type. The Dark type has no role. Since 1.1, Ground-types are Cultivators (R72).
- Create valve handles. Create turns a valve handle only by a fixed angle, so a valve handle cannot give continuous power.
- Work by party Cobblemon or by Cobblemon that a player sent out, except a Quick Work order (R63, the QWK rows). Outside a
  Quick Work order, only pastured Cobblemon work.
- Effects of Work Stamina on battles.
- Level scaling for roles other than the Generator, Pump, Muse, Telekinesis, Magnet, Tailwind and, in 1.1, the Cultivator, and the
  Superheater's level gate.
- A harvest into a container. The Harvester drops its harvest on the ground beside the crop, where a Magnet collects it (R73).
- Crop families outside the adapter table of ADR-0015 as default targets, for example saplings, trees, sugar cane, cactus,
  bamboo, kelp, mushrooms and flowers. A pack adds a block to a tag of the farming roles, and a crop adapter then reads it
  (CD-CRP-03).
- An output cap beyond the values in role files and the server config. A pack that raises a rate gets that rate.
- A server-only install. The mod adds items, blocks and screens, so clients need it too.
- Work suitability tables per species. Eligibility comes from the types in role files, and role files hold no species lists.
