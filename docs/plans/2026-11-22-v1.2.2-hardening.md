### Task 1: wide workers at the pasture block

**The fault.** In a GameTest run, an unbound Torterra 2.25 blocks wide stopped beside its pasture block for 600 ticks and missed its
arrival. A leashed worker then waits 6,000 ticks in Standby (CD-ASG-17). The fix of 1.2.1 for a stuck traveler moves only a worker
narrower than a block (CD-ASG-11, `Unstick.middle`).

**The cause.** Cobblemon's pathfinder checks at most 2 cells across for a mob: `OmniPathNodeMaker.getPathTypeOfMob` clamps the
width in cells, `(int)(maxX - minX) + 1`, with `coerceAtMost(2)`. Navigation steers a mob to `node + (int)(bbWidth + 1) / 2` on x
and z (`Path.getEntityPosAtNode`), which is `node + 1.5` for a mob 2 to 3 blocks wide. The body of a mob 2.25 wide then spans
`node + 0.375` to `node + 2.625`, so it reaches 0.625 blocks into a third row of cells on the east or south side, which no path
checks. A path beside the pasture block can put the block in that row. The body then walks straight into the block's face, and no
sideways part of the move lets it slide past. The navigation reports it stuck and plans the same path again. A trace of the scene
from the east: the Torterra walked west along z = 10.51 and stopped at x = 13.00 for 480 ticks, with its west face on the block's
east face (x = 11.875) and the block's top half (z 11.0625 to 11.625) inside its body's span (z 9.385 to 11.635).

**The fix (CD-ASG-27).** A stuck traveler 1 block wide or wider moves sideways by the shortest distance that clears the blocks
on a face of its body that leads at least a tenth of the way toward its path's next point. The step is at most half its width, so
its center stays inside the space that its body held. The new place needs room for the body and no block ahead of that face, its
center inside the work area over a sturdy floor, and a walkable or open cell with no fluid under each part of the body at its feet,
as the free cells of CD-ASG-25 have. A worker that rides or carries another entity does not step. The worker must push against a
block (`Entity.horizontalCollision`) on the stall of 1.2.1 (100 ticks within 0.25 blocks), on a short watch (20 ticks in a row of
pushing within 0.1 blocks) or while navigation reports it stuck. A block whose check throws leaves the worker where it stands, with
one warning for each cause. The cost per tick is the short watch's arithmetic for each traveler 1 block wide or wider.
`WorkerSlot.lastSidestepTick` limits the collision queries to at most one check in 20 ticks for each worker, also while
navigation keeps reporting it stuck.

**Rejected.** A mixin that removes Cobblemon's clamp for workers. The claim's path check (CD-ASG-09) would then refuse many work
spots that wide workers reach today, because the spot rule puts the body on the spot's center and the pathfinder puts it at the
spot's corner.

- [x] **Step 1: Repro.** Torterra workers at their species' size (2.25 wide) and at 0.533 times it (1.2 wide) walk past the pasture
  block to an oak sapling from each side, on 5 layouts a side, 20 copies each. Before the fix, 4 layouts of the 2.25-wide Torterra
  failed in 20 of 20 runs: from the east and the south with the path 1 block off the pasture's row, and from the north and the west
  with the path 2 blocks off it (and the sapling 4 or 5 blocks past the pasture). Every other 2.25-wide layout arrived in 20 of 20.
  The 1.2-wide Torterra walks 0.012 blocks a tick, half the speed of the 2.25-wide one, so most of its routes past the pasture
  take more than 600 ticks with no stall. Its counts did not change with the fix.
- [x] **Step 2: Unit tests.** `UnstickTest`: `Unstick.sideShifts` and the short watch of `TravelStall`.
- [x] **Step 3: Fix.** `Unstick.sideShifts`, `TravelStall(PRESS_TICKS, PRESS_RADIUS)`, `WorkerSlot.press`, and
  `WorkEngine.sidestep`.
- [x] **Step 4: GameTests.** `WidePastureTests`: the 4 failing layouts, one test each. After the fix, each passed in 20 of 20 runs,
  the slowest 521 ticks after its claim.
- [x] **Step 5: Spec.** CD-ASG-27, its trace line under R81, the failure sweep, and the counts of the Checks section.
- [x] **Step 6: Review findings.** A sturdy floor under the new center and safe cells under the whole body, no step for a rider
  or a carrier, the work area, one check in 20 ticks, a guard for blocks whose checks throw, pushing on every trigger and on every
  tick of the short watch, no speed after the step, and a face must lead a tenth of the way. Unit tests in `UnstickTest`. A cell
  whose block fills only part of it, such as the pasture block's, reads `BLOCKED`, and the body's collision check already keeps the
  body out of that block, so only fluids and hazards fail a cell under the body. Cobblemon's default pasture state is waterlogged,
  unlike a pasture that a player places on dry land. With a dry pasture, each scene failed in 20 of 20 runs on the engine of 1.2.1
  and passed in 20 of 20 with the fix, the slowest 505 ticks after its claim.
- [x] **Step 7: Land beside water.** Farms have water channels by their rows, so a cell that reads `WATER_BORDER` counts as a
  walkable center and foot for the step, and as a free cell for CD-ASG-25 (`Unstick.isOpenFoot`). A cell with open fluid still
  fails. Water inside a block with collision, such as a waterlogged pasture block, does not fail a cell (`Unstick.isDryFoot`),
  because the body's collision check keeps the body out of that block. The scene from the east keeps the waterlogged default
  pasture, and the other three dry it.
