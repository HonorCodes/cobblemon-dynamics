# Cobblemon Dynamics 1.1: the Cultivator, the Harvester and modded crops

> Read this index first, then the task that you execute. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship Cobblemon Dynamics 1.1.0 with two farming roles that work vanilla crops and the crops of the COHO 2.5.0
modpack. Ground-types are Cultivators: they grow crops with the bonemeal effect, using Work Stamina and no item.
Grass-types are Harvesters: they harvest fully grown crops, drop the harvest on the ground beside the crop, and replant
from the drops. Both go crop to crop with no leash, or wait at one crop with a leash, and both take Quick Work orders.

**Architecture:** Two new target handlers, `CultivatorHandler` and `HarvesterHandler`, scan blocks as the Crystal Tender
does. Two block tags decide which blocks each role works. Crop adapters decide how: a pure rule picks an adapter from plain
facts of a block (its class flags, its adapter tags, its `age`), and a pure planner turns the loot into drops and cell
changes. A thin layer reads those facts from the level and applies the plan with silent block updates. The claim registry
keys a farming claim by the crop and the role's lane, so a Cultivator and a Harvester share a crop. No class of a crop
mod is named: the adapters read vanilla classes, Cobblemon's classes, block state properties and tags.

**Tech Stack:** The stack of the [Phase 3 plan](2026-11-11-phase-3-release.md): NeoForge 21.1.265, Minecraft 1.21.1,
Cobblemon 1.8.4, Kotlin for Forge, Create 6.0.10 and AE2 19.2.19 for the GameTests. 1.1 adds no dependency. The crop mods
of COHO 2.5.0 load only in the test pack's `crops` server and on the COHO server.

**Spec:** The [spec sheet](2026-11-09-v1-spec-sheet.md): the areas CUL (CD-CUL-01 to CD-CUL-10), HRV (CD-HRV-01 to
CD-HRV-15) and CRP (CD-CRP-01 to CD-CRP-22), the rows CD-BAL-08, CD-BAL-09 and CD-REL-16, and the 1.1 parts of the rows of
[Rows of earlier releases with a 1.1 part](#rows-of-earlier-releases-with-a-11-part). Inputs R72, R73, RES8, BM4 and BM5
hold the rulings, the research and the benchmarks. Design: [ADR-0015](../adr/0015-farming-roles-and-crop-adapters.md),
whose adapter table lists every crop family. If this plan and the spec sheet disagree, the spec sheet wins. Fix the plan
in the same pull request.

## Test approach

The approver wants fast, confident tests: unit tests for the pure rules, and few GameTests. This plan tests the farming
roles in four layers, from the cheapest to the dearest:

1. **Pure rules, unit tests.** Every decision that the adapters make is a pure function of plain values, as
   `FtbInteractRule` is for FTB Chunks. `CropFacts` holds what a rule reads of a block: its class flags, the adapter tags
   that hold it, its `age` with its lowest and highest values, its halves, its berries flag. The tests build facts for
   each family of the adapter table of ADR-0015 from the values that RES8 read with `javap`, so the bytecode research is
   the oracle and no game starts. The rules: the choice of the adapter (`CropAdapters`), full growth and the Cultivator's
   demand (`CropRules`), the harvest plan with the replant (`HarvestPlans`), the face of the drops (`DropFace`), the pace
   (`GrowthPace`), the stall (`GrowthStall`), the empty cell (`PlotRule`), the lanes of the claim registry, the release of
   the worker machine, the Quick Work rules and the role picks.
2. **Data tests, unit tests.** The tag files and role files are data. `FarmingTagFilesTest` checks that every family of
   the adapter table is in its tags, that every entry of another mod has `required` false, that no apricorn or terrain seed
   is a default target, and that `#minecraft:bee_growables` stays out. The role file tests read the numbers, and
   `SilenceTest` and `CompatImportsTest` scan the sources.
3. **Six GameTests, vanilla crops only, in the main run.** They prove the glue that a unit test cannot: real `CropBlock`s,
   real loot tables, the engine's claims and leashes, the Magnet, the claim gate and Quick Work. They are the scenes of
   CD-CRP-10, CD-HRV-07, CD-CRP-11, CD-HRV-13 (two tests) and CD-CRP-12.
4. **The real crop mods, with no GameTest run.** The test pack's `crops` case starts a dedicated server with the crop
   mods of COHO 2.5.0 and reads the load lines of CD-CRP-05: every tagged block of the defaults must resolve to its adapter,
   with an empty "no adapter" list. That catches a renamed block, a lost tag and a changed class in seconds, on real jars.
   The behaviour of each modded family then gets one Demo on the COHO server (check 62 of the manual checklist), where the
   approver plays. A GameTest run with the crop mods, as the claim runs have, is design decision 10.

## Global Constraints

The [Global Constraints of the Phase 2 plan](2026-11-10-phase-2-plan.md#global-constraints) and the
[Phase 3 additions](2026-11-11-phase-3-release.md#global-constraints) hold. 1.1 adds these lines:

- No class of a crop mod in the main source set. Only Cobblemon's classes and vanilla classes (CD-CRP-19).
- The farming handlers post no level event, play no sound, and call neither `Level.destroyBlock` nor a block's use method
  (CD-CRP-14). They change blocks with `Level.setBlock` and drop items with `Block.popResourceFromFace`.
- No fake player and no break event. The harvest computes loot with `Block.getDrops` and an empty tool.
- The Harvester asks the land claim checks in the tick of each harvest, for every block that it changes (CD-HRV-13).
- The farming numbers live in the role files. 1.1 adds no key to the server config (CD-CFG-01 and its key list do not
  change).
- 1.1 adds no network payload, so the payload version stays 3 (CD-CMP-09).
- Player-facing text calls the creatures Cobblemon.

What the bytecode decides (`javap -c`):

1. `CropBlock.isValidBonemealTarget` is `!isMaxAge(state)`, `isBonemealSuccess` is true, and `performBonemeal` adds 2 to 5
   ages, capped at `getMaxAge()`.
2. `BoneMealItem.applyBonemeal` fires `BonemealEvent(player, level, pos, state, stack)` first. On a valid target of a
   `ServerLevel`, it calls `performBonemeal` when `isBonemealSuccess` passes, then `stack.shrink(1)`, and returns true.
   `growCrop` passes a null player, so a null player is the dispenser's path.
3. `BoneMealItem.useOn` posts level event 1505, and `LevelRenderer.levelEvent` case 1505 runs `addGrowthParticles` and then
   `playLocalSound(pos, SoundEvents.BONE_MEAL_USE, ...)`. So the Cultivator sends `happy_villager` particles itself.
4. `Level.destroyBlock` posts level event 2001, whose client case plays the block's break sound. The harvest never calls it.
5. `SweetBerryBushBlock.useWithoutItem` plays `SWEET_BERRY_BUSH_PICK_BERRIES`, and `CaveVines.use` plays
   `CAVE_VINES_PICK_BERRIES`. A pick sets the state itself.
6. `BerryBlockEntity.harvest(level, state, pos, player)` groups the berry stacks, fires `BerryHarvestEvent` only for a
   `ServerPlayer`, and calls `refresh`, which sets age 3 with flag 2 and posts the game event with the nullable player. It
   plays no sound.
7. `FarmBlock.fallOn` calls `CommonHooks.onFarmlandTrample`, which posts `FarmlandTrampleEvent` before `turnToDirt`.
8. `Block.popResourceFromFace` spawns the item at the centre of the face, ±0.25, moving out of the face, with the default
   pickup delay, and only while `doTileDrops` is on.
9. `Items.WHEAT_SEEDS` is `new ItemNameBlockItem(Blocks.WHEAT, ...)`, so `Blocks.WHEAT.asItem()` is wheat seeds.
10. `Minecraft.startUseItem` sets `rightClickDelay` to 4: a player who holds bone meal applies it every 4 ticks (BM5).

enum class HarvestKind(val tag: String?) {
    NEVER("harvest/never"),
    PICK_TO_AGE_0("harvest/pick_to_age_0"),
    PICK_TO_AGE_1("harvest/pick_to_age_1"),
    UPPER_HALF("harvest/upper_half"),
    BREAK_AND_REPLANT("harvest/break_and_replant"),
    BERRY_TREE(null),
    CAVE_VINES(null),
    GOURD(null),
    CROP(null),
}

/** What the adapters read of one block state. [CropFactsReader] fills it from a level, and the unit tests build it by hand. */
data class CropFacts(
    val adapterTags: Set<HarvestKind> = emptySet(),
    /** `ApricornBlock`, or `#cobblemon:apricorns`. */
    val apricorn: Boolean = false,
    val cropBlock: Boolean = false,
    val bonemealable: Boolean = false,
    /** Cobblemon's `BerryBlock`. */
    val berryTree: Boolean = false,
    val caveVines: Boolean = false,
    val attachedStem: Boolean = false,
    /** The value of the `IntegerProperty` named `age`, with its lowest and highest possible values, or null with no such property. */
    val age: Int? = null,
    val ageMin: Int = 0,
    val ageMax: Int = 0,
    /** `CropBlock.isMaxAge`, for a `CropBlock`. */
    val cropMaxAge: Boolean = false,
    /** The state has `half` = upper. */
    val upperHalf: Boolean = false,
    /** A lower half with its upper half above it. */
    val upperPresent: Boolean = false,
    /** `CaveVines.BERRIES`. */
    val berries: Boolean = false,
)

object CropAdapters {
    /** CD-CRP-03, CD-HRV-03: the first harvest adapter that claims the block, or null for none (CD-CRP-04). */
    fun harvestKind(f: CropFacts): HarvestKind? = when {
        f.apricorn || HarvestKind.NEVER in f.adapterTags -> null
        else -> HarvestKind.entries.firstOrNull { it.tag != null && it in f.adapterTags } ?: when {
            f.berryTree -> HarvestKind.BERRY_TREE
            f.caveVines -> HarvestKind.CAVE_VINES
            f.attachedStem -> HarvestKind.GOURD
            f.cropBlock -> HarvestKind.CROP
            else -> null
        }
    }

    /** CD-CUL-02: the growth adapter claims every `BonemealableBlock`. */
    fun grows(f: CropFacts): Boolean = f.bonemealable
}

object CropRules {
    /** CD-HRV-04. */
    fun isFullyGrown(kind: HarvestKind, f: CropFacts): Boolean = when (kind) {
        HarvestKind.NEVER -> false
        HarvestKind.PICK_TO_AGE_0, HarvestKind.PICK_TO_AGE_1 -> f.age != null && f.age == f.ageMax
        HarvestKind.UPPER_HALF -> f.age != null && f.age == f.ageMax && f.upperPresent
        HarvestKind.BREAK_AND_REPLANT -> f.age == null || f.age == f.ageMax
        // Cobblemon's FRUIT_AGE is 5, the top of the berry tree's age property (RES8), so no Cobblemon class loads in a unit test.
        HarvestKind.BERRY_TREE -> f.age != null && f.age == f.ageMax
        HarvestKind.CAVE_VINES -> f.berries
        HarvestKind.GOURD -> true
        HarvestKind.CROP -> f.cropMaxAge
    }

    /** CD-CUL-03: bonemeal can still change the crop, and the Harvester would not take it yet. */
    fun cultivatorDemand(validTarget: Boolean, kind: HarvestKind?, f: CropFacts): Boolean =

/** What a harvest does to one cell. [Clear] leaves the cell's fluid, air or water. */
sealed interface CellChange {
    data object Keep : CellChange
    data object Clear : CellChange
    data class SetAge(val age: Int) : CellChange
    data object ClearBerries : CellChange
    /** The crop's first growth state (CD-HRV-07). */
    data object Replant : CellChange
    /** The first state of another block that a dropped seed places, such as the Aether's stem. */
    data class ReplantAs(val block: ResourceLocation) : CellChange
}

    val fruit: CellChange = CellChange.Keep,

     * the Cultivator's tag to that block. [survives] tells whether a replant of that block can survive in the crop's cell, where
     * null is the crop's own first state.

## Design decisions for the approver

The rulings of R72 and R73 settle the features. This plan made these further calls. The coordinator rules on them under
the approver's delegation, except calls 3 and 4, which are Q10 and Q11 of the spec sheet and go to the approver:

1. **Adapters: tags and classes, no crop-mod code.** Five adapter tags and four class adapters cover every family of COHO
   2.5.0, and a pack extends them with a datapack. The other forms were a table in code, which a pack cannot extend, or one
   JSON file per family, which needs a new loader. The JSON files stay open for a later version.
2. **The defaults leave out** `#minecraft:bee_growables` and `#cobblemon:crops` as a whole, Cobblemon's terrain seeds and
   roots, Farmer's Delight's mushroom colonies and wild crops, Pam's gardens and saplings, the Legendary Monuments carrots,
   Supplementaries' rope and stick tomatoes, and the plants of My Nether's Delight and Ender's Delight. Each one is listed
   with its reason in ADR-0015. A pack can add any of them.
3. **Apricorns and the Cultivator (Q10, for the approver).** The approver excluded apricorns from the harvest only. This
   plan leaves them out of the Cultivator's default tag too, so they keep their natural pace.
4. **Pace and cost (Q11, for the approver).** ceil(30 × L ÷ 100) uses per minute and 100 ticks of Work Stamina per use. A
   level 100 Cultivator works about 22% of the time with no bed and about 70% on a Starlit Bed, and applies a tenth of a
   player's bone meal rate.
5. **Farming claims by lane.** A crop holds one claim of each farming role, and a binding reserves only its own role's
   lane. The other form, one claim per crop, keeps a waiting Cultivator's crop away from the Harvester.
6. **Crop to crop in 20 ticks.** An auto-assigned farming worker releases its claim within 20 ticks of the end of its
   crop's demand, a farming Quick Work order ends within 20 ticks, and 20 growth uses with no change stop a crop's demand
   for 6,000 ticks.
7. **Land claims: the use question, and the harvest tick (ruling 6 kept as given).** The Harvester asks the pasture check
   of 1.0.0, which asks whether the placer may right-click, and it asks again in the tick of each harvest for every block
   that changes. A pick is a right-click, so the check is exact for picks. For a harvest that breaks a block, FTB Chunks has
   a separate block edit mode and MineColonies a separate `BREAK_BLOCKS` action. COHO's FTB default sets both modes to
   allies, so the answers agree, but a team that sets its edit mode to private still lets an ally's Harvester break its
   crops. Recommendation: add a break question to `LandClaimCheck` for break harvests (FTB Chunks `Protection.EDIT_BLOCK`,
   MineColonies `BREAK_BLOCKS`; Open Parties and Claims' full access already covers both), in a later task or in 1.1. It
   needs `javap` research of both mods, and the approver's ruling on the scope.
8. **The empty cell keeps a binding.** A player's harvest by hand leaves air, which CD-LSH-08 treats as a broken target.
   CD-CRP-09 keeps the binding while the cell holds air or a fluid above a block.
9. **The drops land on the crop's face toward the work spot.** That is beside the crop, toward the worker, inside the
   work area for the Magnet, and outside bushes that damage items.
10. **No GameTest run with the crop mods.** The modded families rest on the unit tests of their facts, the test pack's load
    lines and the Demo of check 62 on the COHO server, by the approver's rulings on tests. The other form is a fourth
    claim-style run, `runGameTestServerCrops`, outside CI, with one GameTest per family: about a day of work and about 30
    library jars to pin.
11. **Picks drop the full loot of the ripe state.** The base of an FD tomato then gives a tomato seed per pick, a duskmelon 1
    to 2 slices where its own pick gives 1, and a galarica nut bush 2 to 3 nuts where its own pick gives 1 to 2. Copying each
    mod's own yield in code would drift from the pack's loot tables.
12. **A Cobblemon that fits both roles** takes, for Quick Work, the role with demand on the aimed crop, and for the Work
    Leash, the role of its primary type.
13. **The effects.** Mud Sport for the Cultivator, with the target particle only, because `mudsport_actor` plays a sound,
    and Razor Leaf for the Harvester. The bonemeal sparkles are vanilla `happy_villager` particles through
    `sendParticles`.
14. **One pull request for 1.1, then the tag.** The tag `v1.1.0` waits for the approver's approval.
15. **MineColonies 1.1.1420 (outside farming).** COHO 2.5.0 moves MineColonies from 1.1.1419 to 1.1.1420, which changed only
    blueprints. CD-LND-16 and the MineColonies run name 1.1.1419. The coordinator decides whether 1.1 re-runs the
    MineColonies GameTests and names 1.1.1420 on the integration page.
16. **Miner's Delight+ may not load.** Its `neoforge.mods.toml` names Minecraft `[1.21,1.21.1)`. Its entry in the tags is
    optional, and the Demo of check 62 shows whether COHO loads it.
