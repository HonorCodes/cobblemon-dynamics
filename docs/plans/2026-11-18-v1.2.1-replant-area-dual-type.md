# Cobblemon Dynamics 1.2.1: a full replant, the farming area, and dual-type workers

> Read this index first, then the task that you execute. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship Cobblemon Dynamics 1.2.1. A Lumberjack always replants its tree, four saplings for a 2 × 2 trunk, and a
Harvester always replants a crop that it breaks. A Cultivator grows, and a Harvester harvests, one more crop next to its
target for each 10 levels. A Cobblemon of two types does the work of each of its roles: on its bound target, on its auto
targets, and in the pick of a Quick Work order. A Cobblemon of both the Bug and the Flying type picks ripe apricorns as the
Picker (R95, [Task E](#task-e-the-picker)).

**Architecture:** No new handler and no new kind of claim. Task A changes the replant rules of `handler/tree/` and
`handler/farming/`. Task B adds one pure area rule, `CropArea`, which both farming handlers call in `applyWork`. Task C
adds one pure rule, `RoleSwitch`, which the engine asks at each poll, and changes the role choice of the auto-assignment and
Quick Work. Each decision stays a pure function of plain facts with a unit test, as in 1.1 and 1.2.

**Tech Stack:** The stack of the [1.2 plan](2026-11-15-v1.2-new-roles-and-compat.md): NeoForge 21.1.265, Minecraft 1.21.1,
Cobblemon 1.8.4 and Kotlin for Forge. 1.2.1 adds no dependency.

## The rulings

Input R94 of the spec sheet holds them verbatim. In short:

- The report: a Lumberjack at a tree with a 2 × 2 base replants one sapling.
- "Always a full 2×2", and "fix it by always replanting even if not enough saplings were dropped; same with crops: even if
  the crop doesn't drop a seed, always replant."
- "+1 crop per 10 levels": each use also grows N other growing crops next to its target, N = level ÷ 10, rounded down.
  "Harvester harvests an area too": N extra fully grown crops per harvest.
- "Yes, any role with work": a bound worker does any of its roles that has work on its target, switching as the target
  changes, and unbound dual-types also take auto work for either type.

The rows of 1.2.1 are `draft` until the coordinator rules on the [design decisions](#design-decisions). Nobody builds against
a `draft` row.

## What 1.2.0 does today

### The report of one sapling

`ReplantRule.choose` (`handler/tree/ReplantRule.kt:27-34`) plants four saplings only when the drops hold four of one kind,
and otherwise one at the corner, or none for a sapling of `lumberjack/needs_four`. The drops come from `Block.getDrops` with
an empty tool, so no Fortune applies: a jungle leaf drops its sapling 1 time in 40, and a spruce or dark oak leaf 1 time in
20. A giant tree often drops fewer than four, and the handler plants one. That one sapling grows a small spruce or jungle
tree, whose next fell replants one again, so after one short fell the bound tree stays small for good. This explains the
report.

No other fault gives one sapling. `FellPlan.twoByTwo` (`FellPlan.kt:51`) takes the square from `tree.base`, which
`TreeSearch.LOWEST` makes the trunk's least cell, so the square is the trunk's own. The vanilla trunk placers put the four
columns of a giant trunk on one level and turn the cells below them to dirt, and the fell sets each trunk cell to its fluid,
so each cell of the square is empty and over dirt when `end` runs. The base cell's survival filter (`ReplantRule.kt:27`)
and the claim re-check (`allowedBy`) give all four or none, never one. A refused cell of the square plants nothing today.

### The failures of a dual-type worker

1. **A Bug/Ground worker leashed to a tree never grows its sapling.** The binding holds the Lumberjack (`Binding.roleId`),
   and `claimBound` claims with that role only (`engine/WorkEngine.kt:431` and `:436`). On a sapling the Lumberjack has no
   demand (`handler/tree/LumberjackHandler.kt:98-103`), so the worker waits in Standby until the sapling grows by random
   ticks.
2. **A Bug/Ground worker leashed by a click on a sapling loses its binding when the tree grows.** Both roles fit a sapling
   (`TreeRules.matches`, `handler/tree/TreeRules.kt:27`). The bind picks by priority, then id (`leash/LeashInteractions.kt:119`),
   and the two roles are no group (`role/RoleRegistry.kt:147`, the Lumberjack has no lane), so `cultivator` wins over
   `lumberjack`. Once the oak grows, the Cultivator's `bindingHolds` (`handler/farming/CultivatorHandler.kt:51`) is false on a
   log, and `LeashResets.check` clears the binding (`leash/LeashResets.kt:199-201`). The tree is never felled.
3. **A click on a sapling of a 2 × 2 that is not its corner names the wrong cell for the Lumberjack.** The Cultivator resolves
   a sapling to itself (`CultivatorHandler.kt:43`), and the Lumberjack resolves it to the square's corner, which becomes the
   giant tree's base (`TreeRules.kt:79-83`). `TreeRules.treeOn` needs the base on the bound cell, so even a switch would find
   no tree there.
4. **A Grass/Ground worker leashed to a crop does one role.** The bind gives the primary type's role (CD-CRP-22,
   `LeashInteractions.kt:119` with `RoleRegistry::sameGroup`), and the fixed role (`WorkEngine.kt:436`) keeps a leashed
   farming worker in Standby (`engine/WorkerMachine.kt:65`). A Torterra never grows its wheat, and a Toedscool never harvests
   it.
5. **An auto Poison/Fire worker never brews.** `Assigner.roleFor` (`engine/Assigner.kt:70-71`) picks by priority with no
   demand, so a Salazzle at a brewing stand always takes the Brew Stoker (priority 10). The candidate's demand is that of any
   role (`WorkEngine.kt:600-604`), so a fuelled stand with a brew looks like work, and the worker then waits in Standby with
   no work. The Standby move leaves out the worker's own target (`Assigner.kt:53`), so the worker walks to another target and
   never speeds the brew. CD-ASG-16 pinned this choice.
6. **A Quick Work order of a Poison/Fire worker at a fuelled stand is refused.** `QuickWorkRules.pick`
   (`engine/QuickWorkRules.kt:104`) asks `RoleSelection.best` with demand, but `best` weighs demand only within the group of
   the first role (`role/RoleSelection.kt:26-28`). The Brew Stoker and the Brewer have other handlers and priorities, so the
   pick is the Brew Stoker, and `refusal` sends "needs no work now" while the Brewer has work.
7. **A Poison/Fire worker leashed to a brewing stand never brews,** for the reasons of 1 and 5.

An unbound Water/Fire worker has no failure of its own with the default role files. No block fits a Water role and a Fire
role except the encased fan, whose three catalyst roles share a handler, are leash only and pick by type (ruling 1 of the
Phase 2 plan). Each scan candidate holds the roles of its own block (`WorkEngine.kt:567-570`), so a Water/Fire worker already
gets the wheels and the furnaces near it. An unbound Grass/Ground worker already grows and harvests a field, because a crop
is one candidate in each lane (CD-CRP-06). CD-ASG-23 pins both. The fault appears only where one block fits roles of both
types, as at a brewing stand, and Task C fixes the pick there.

Minecraft or Cobblemon is uncertain, and no client test. Each task marks its other GameTests "GameTest deferred (high
confidence)", and [Task D](#task-d-the-121-gate-and-the-release) lists them for `scripts/gate_report.py`. The new GameTests:

- Task A: the real leaf loot tables (CD-LUM-31), a real 2 × 2 jungle fell (CD-LUM-18) and the seed items of real crops
  (CD-HRV-22).
- Task B: the area of a real Cultivator in the engine, with its work area and lanes (CD-CUL-11).
- Task C: the Bug/Ground cycle at a real oak (CD-LSH-30, CD-LSH-31) and a leashed Torterra at real wheat (CD-CRP-22).

Each task puts its GameTests in a new file of its own, so the three branches share no test file.

## Global Constraints

The Global Constraints of the [1.2 plan](2026-11-15-v1.2-new-roles-and-compat.md#global-constraints) hold. 1.2.1 adds:

- No new network payload, no new config key and no change of the saved data: a `Binding` keeps its codec and its stored
  role.
- The two new numbers, `levels_per_extra_crop` and `max_extra_crops`, live in `cultivator.json` and `harvester.json`.
- A call into another mod's block or loot table that can throw, such as `getCloneItemStack`, `canSurvive` or a loot roll,
  sits in its own `try` and logs once (CD-LUM-29).
- `SilenceTest` scans `handler/tree/`, `handler/farming/` and the engine: no KDoc may name the methods that it bans.

  `cdAsg26PlayerGrowsATreeOnAWaitingLumberjack` failed on `d93ead6` with the Caterpie in the owner's new oak.
- [x] **The snap.** A worker that arrives at a spot where its body no longer fits gets no snap: it leaves the tree, and a pasture
  worker ends its claim and claims again with a new spot. The net ends the claim of a pasture worker whose spot a tree filled too.
- [x] **The way out.** `free` takes the cells of `Unstick.reachableCells`, a flood fill within the radius through empty cells and
  the tree's logs, leaves and roots, so no cell behind a wall and no far work spot. A cell needs a floor whose top face is sturdy
  (as `LevelSpotGrid.canStand`), no fluid, a path type of `WALKABLE` or `OPEN` (`WalkNodeEvaluator.getPathTypeStatic`), room with
  `noBlockCollision` out of every sapling's column, the work area, and `LandClaims.ownerMayUse` for the owner of the pasture or the
  order.
- [x] **The stop box.** `GrowthColumns.allows` tests both boxes of `WorkerBody.boxes` already: the stop box of a body 1 block wide
  or wider is the second box. A unit test now pins a case where only the stop box reaches the sapling.
- [x] **The square after a fell.** The Lumberjack remembers the base of each 2 × 2 trunk that a fell starts on, at most 4,096, and
  its growth cells keep the whole square after the fell, also when the replant planted the corner alone.

## Design decisions

The coordinator accepted the default of each decision below on 2026-11-18, under the approver's delegation (R81), and set the
31 rows of 1.2.1 to `agreed`. A Quick Work order keeps the role of its pick and does not switch roles.

The coordinator rules on each, under the approver's delegation, before Task A. Each holds the recommended default.

1. **The area's cells.** The cells within 2 blocks along x and z and 1 along y, nearest first (CD-CRP-23). A dense field at
   N = 10 gives the 8 cells around the target and 2 of the next ring, and a terrace counts. Alternatives: the 8 cells around
   the target only, which leaves N = 9 and 10 with nothing more; or 3 blocks along x and z, 146 cells, which reaches past
   "next to its target". Default: 2 and 1.
2. **A crop that another worker holds in the same lane.** Skipped (CD-CRP-24). The lane promises one Cultivator and one
   Harvester per crop (CD-CRP-06), and a Harvester that walks to a crop must find it there. A claim in the other lane does not
   stop the area. Alternative: work it anyway, which lets an area steal a walking worker's harvest. Default: skip.
3. **The cost of a use.** One `stamina_ticks_per_use`, whatever the area grows (CD-CUL-12). At level 100 a Cultivator then
   grows up to 330 crop uses per minute, about 1.1 times a player with bone meal (CD-BAL-08). Alternative: a cost per crop,
   which makes a level 100 Cultivator tire 11 times as fast and cancels the ruling. Default: one cost.
4. **The numbers.** `levels_per_extra_crop` 10 and `max_extra_crops` 10 in both role files (CD-CRP-25). The Botany Pots roles
   keep one pot. Default: these.
5. **The created sapling's kind.** The leaf's loot table is rolled with fixed seeds, at most 512 times, and cached for each
   leaf until a reload (CD-LUM-31). A jungle leaf at 1 in 40 misses all 512 rolls with a chance of about 1 in 400,000, and the
   name rule then covers it. Alternative: read the table's entries through an access transformer, which ties the build to
   private fields of `LootPool`. Default: the roll.
6. **A square with a refused cell.** One sapling at the corner, unless the kind needs four (CD-LUM-18). In 1.2.0 a refused
   cell planted nothing at all. Alternative: nothing. Default: the corner.
7. **The seed item of a crop.** The clone item, else `Block.asItem()`, and a seed of another block of the Cultivator's tag in
   the loot still wins over a created seed of the crop's own block (CD-HRV-22), so the Aether's stems keep their replant.
   Default: this order.
8. **The switch roles.** A leash-only role counts for a bound worker; a role of the bound role's own handler does not; a
   two-target or logistics binding never switches (CD-LSH-33). Default: these.
9. **The lanes of a switch.** A switch takes only a free lane and never preempts (CD-LSH-32), and the binding keeps reserving
   its own role's lane only. Alternative: a switch takes the lane as a new binding does (CD-CLM-05), which lets a Grass/Ground
   worker push another player's bound Cultivator off a shared crop. Default: free lanes only.
10. **The stored role.** A binding keeps the role that its bind picked: the codec, the leash index, the overview and the lane
    reservation stay as in 1.2.0, and no saved data changes. The switch is runtime state of the slot. Default: keep.
11. **The binding holds through a switch role** (CD-LSH-31). Without it, a Bug/Ground binding made on a sapling clears when
    the oak grows. Default: holds.
12. **The Cultivator's sapling corner** (CD-CUL-13). Without it, a binding made on a sapling that is not the square's corner
    names a cell where the Lumberjack never finds the giant tree's base. A side effect: a scan finds one Cultivator target for
    a square of saplings, not four. Default: the corner.
13. **Quick Work keeps its role.** The pick takes a role with demand first (CD-QWK-03), but an order never switches. An order
    is one job (CD-CRP-12), and a switch at a crop would never end: grow, harvest, replant, grow. Alternative: switch as a
    bound worker does, until the owner walks away. Default: no switch. This is the one decision that the approver may want to
    see, because R94 does not name Quick Work.
14. **The auto switch in place** (CD-ASG-21). At the next poll of a worker in Standby, within 20 ticks. A farming worker
    never needs it, because it releases its crop. Default: at the poll.
15. **The probe memos** (CD-LSH-34). One memo for each switch role for the life of the binding, so the Lumberjack's tree wait
    of CD-LUM-28 also bounds the switch's demand checks. Default: kept.
16. **A form change** (CD-LSH-14) still picks among the roles of the bound role's handler. A switch role does not keep a
    binding whose role no longer fits. Default: unchanged.
17. **The gate.** `build`, `runGameTestServer`, `runGameTestServerPerf` and the cases `smoke`, `crops` and `mods`, with no core,
    compat or claim run and no play-test note (CD-REL-18). Default: this gate.
18. **Saplings out of the area** (coordinator's ruling, 2026-11-19). Only crops are cells of the area, and a sapling grows only as a
    target (CD-CUL-11, CD-CRP-24). A square of saplings would grow up to four times in one use, and a tree could grow out of the work
    area or a land claim.
19. **The review of Task B** (2026-11-19). Each crop of the area keeps its own stall: a growth use that changes nothing gives its place
    to the next cell and counts toward the crop's stall of CD-CUL-09 (`ctx.stalledAt`), and a refused harvest of the area waits as a
    refused target. A cell that one area worked in a tick is not worked again by another area of its lane in that tick. A block whose
    check or work throws is left out of the role's area until the server stops, with one warning, and a harvest drops its items once it
    changes a cell, even when a later change throws. A crop of the area needs the cell above it, and a gourd its fruit, inside the work
    area too. A use that changes nothing can call up to 73 growth uses in one tick until those crops stall, still within the 74 cell
    reads of CD-CRP-20.
20. **The Brew Stoker lights a brew** (CD-BRF-04, CD-BRF-03, CD-ASG-16). Ruled by the coordinator on 2026-11-19 under the
    approver's delegation (R81), after a review of Task C. Vanilla resets a stand's `brewTime` unless it can brew, so whenever the
    Brewer had demand the Brew Stoker had too, and the priority gave a Poison/Fire worker the Brew Stoker at every stand. The Brew
    Stoker now has demand only while a brew waits to start: the stand can brew, `brewTime` is 0 and its fuel is below 1. A running
    brew needs no fuel, so a Poison/Fire worker lights a brew as the Brew Stoker, speeds it as the Brewer, and lights the next. A
    Fire-only Brew Stoker waits in Standby while a brew runs, which is accepted: an idle stoker may serve another stand. A stand
    with blaze powder fuels itself, and the Brew Stoker has no demand there.
21. **Quick Work orders the Picker** (CD-PCK-12). Ruled by the coordinator's default on 2026-11-19 under the approver's delegation
    (R81, R95), by the logic that keeps a leash-only role out of Quick Work: Quick Work never takes a role that needs the Work
    Leash, and the Picker needs none. It is an auto-assign role whose targets a scan visits and which names one target, so an order
    is a short auto claim that its owner aims. The order ends after its pick, as a farming order does (CD-CRP-12). Alternative: no
    Quick Work, with `quick_work` false in the file, which would make the role the only auto role with neither the leash nor Quick
    Work for no gain. Default: orders.
22. **Both land claim questions** (CD-PCK-09). A pick takes a block's loot as a break does, so the Picker asks the change and then
    the break of each cell, where the Harvester asks the break only of the cells that it breaks. Alternative: the change only, as the
    Harvester's pick of a bush. Default: both, as the coordinator's default names them.
23. **The refusal of a leash click** (CD-PCK-04). A click where only a role with no leash fits says "{Cobblemon} works here on its
    own, with no Work Leash" in place of "No role fits", which would tell the player that the Butterfree cannot pick apricorns at
    all. A role file with `auto_assign` false and `leash` false does not load unless it opts in to Quick Work, because nothing could
    give that role.
24. **Two optional keys of the role file** (CD-PCK-02, CD-PCK-04). `type_match` (`any` or `all`) and `leash` (true or false) leave
    `types` a list of names, so every file of 1.2.0 and of a pack reads as before and the codec of a binding does not change.
    Alternative: a list of lists of types, which changes the meaning of `types` for every pack.
25. **The fruit of a pack and the claim lane** (CD-PCK-05, CD-PCK-07, CD-PCK-10). A block of the tag that is no `ApricornBlock`
    drops its loot with no tool and takes its lowest `age` with flag 2, as Cobblemon's `harvest` does for an apricorn. The Picker's
    claims have no lane, so a fruit takes one Picker, and no other role of the default files matches an apricorn.
