# Cobblemon Dynamics 1.2: new roles, the Experience Collector, and the machines of other mods

> Read this index first, then the task that you execute. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship Cobblemon Dynamics 1.2.0. Bug-types fell and replant trees as Lumberjacks, with a Work Leash. Ghost-types
collect XP orbs into a new Experience Collector block as Soul Collectors, and the collector gives the XP to a player through
a button or to pipes. Ice-types freeze surface water as Freezers. Fire-types heat the machines of Mekanism and PneumaticCraft
as Heaters. The existing roles also work the machines and crops of the supported mods of ADR-0016, with no setup by the
server admin. Seven fixes correct today's behaviour.

**Architecture:** Three new handlers in vanilla code (`handler/tree/`, `handler/xp/`, `handler/water/`) and a new block
(`xp/`). Each handler keeps its decisions in pure rules over plain facts, as the crop adapters of 1.1 do. A thin layer reads
the facts from the level and applies the result with silent block updates. Every class that names an optional mod
lives in its own compat package and mixin package, which loads only with that mod. A role of such a mod is its own role file
with a `neoforge:mod_loaded` condition, which the role registry now honours.

**Tech Stack:** The stack of the [1.1 plan](2026-11-12-v1.1-farming.md): NeoForge 21.1.265, Minecraft 1.21.1, Cobblemon
1.8.4, Kotlin for Forge, Create 6.0.10 and AE2 19.2.19 for the GameTests. 1.2 adds the compile-only dependencies of
[Mod pins](#mod-pins). The real mods load only in the test pack's case `mods`, the compat GameTest run and the COHO server.

**Spec:** The [spec sheet](2026-11-09-v1-spec-sheet.md): the areas OPT (CD-OPT-01 to CD-OPT-17), FIX (CD-FIX-01 to
CD-FIX-07), LUM (CD-LUM-01 to CD-LUM-30), XPC (CD-XPC-01 to CD-XPC-16), SOU (CD-SOU-01 to CD-SOU-11), FRZ (CD-FRZ-01 to
CD-FRZ-14), HTR (CD-HTR-01 to CD-HTR-07), FUEL (CD-FUEL-01 to CD-FUEL-14), AIR (CD-AIR-01 to CD-AIR-08), KIN (CD-KIN-01 to
CD-KIN-06), FRM (CD-FRM-01 to CD-FRM-08) and MCH (CD-MCH-01 to CD-MCH-12), the rows CD-BAL-10 to CD-BAL-17, CD-CFG-20 and
CD-REL-17, and the 1.2 parts of the rows of [Rows of earlier releases with a 1.2 part](#rows-of-earlier-releases-with-a-12-part).

### The coordinator, 2026-11-15, under the approver's delegation (R79)

- **The Lumberjack (Bug).** A Work Leash role only, with no auto-assignment and no Quick Work. The binding goes to a tree and
  resolves to its trunk base cell. The worker fells the whole tree with that tree's own natural leaves, a `LeavesBlock` whose
  `PERSISTENT` is false, also where the tree rises above the work area. It replants a sapling from the drops at once. The
  drops land beside the trunk for a Magnet, and the binding then waits on the sapling, as a bound Harvester waits. Never:
  player-placed leaves, Pam's fruit trees (the blocks of `harvest/pick_to_age_0`), apricorn trees, the Twilight Forest's
  magic trees. Skip huge nether fungi and trees over 256 logs. Ask each claim mod's break question for each block. Attached
  blocks need a rule, and the design proposes the open details.
- **Saplings.** `#minecraft:saplings` joins the Cultivator's tag, so a Ground-type and a bound Bug-type run a wood line.
- **The Soul Collector (Ghost).** A Work Leash role only, bound only to an Experience Collector, with no bottle mode and no
  direct target in the tanks of other mods. It takes the XP orbs of the pasture's work area, but leaves every orb within 8
  blocks of a player. Its pace grows with level, and it costs Work Stamina.
- **The Experience Collector.** A block of this mod whose front faces the placer. A right-click opens a screen with the stored
  XP in points and levels and a "Collect experience" button, and a request that the server checks gives all the XP to that
  player. The back and the bottom are drain-only outputs. Pipes see the XP as the fluid of the installed mod with no setup:
  `create_enchantment_industry:experience` at 1 mB per XP first, otherwise the first fluid of `c:experience` at 20 mB per XP.
  Check Applied Experienced's import through experiencelib, and add it where it works. No fluid of our own, no insert by
  pipes, the XP kept on the item when broken, a comparator signal, a capacity key, a vanilla recipe, collection by anyone who
  can open it, protection by the claim mods as a block, and original art.
- **The Freezer (Ice).** Automatic in Work Mode pastures, with no Quick Work. The worker freezes the water sources with air
  above them within 5 blocks of itself, only on top, and walks on the new ice. It skips water that hydrates farmland, the top
  of a bubble column and sources that feed a flow. Each cell asks the break question. Vanilla's melting stays.
- **The machines of other mods.** M1 to M8 of ADR-0020, with their mechanisms, numbers and versions from RES10.
- **Zero setup.** Every supported mod works out of the box with built-in values. Admins never need config or data packs.
  Roles and targets of an optional mod switch on only with that mod. Cross-mod balance may be rough, but every new role or
  target gets a value and a benchmark. The Work Leash rules stay as they are.
- **Cut:** the Rancher, every fan catalyst of Create: Dragons Plus and the Quencher. Not added: sanding by Ground-types,
  coke ovens and the AE2 Vibration Chamber.
- **Version** 1.2.0. **Tests** stay light: unit tests, one GameTest run, and one check with the real mod for each newly
  supported mod in the isolated test pack, not in CI.

## Test approach

The approver wants fast, confident tests. 1.2 tests in five layers, from the cheapest to the dearest. This is the proposal of
ruling 6 of R80, and design decision 31 holds it.

1. **Pure rules, unit tests.** Every decision of the new handlers and of each adapter of another mod is a pure function of
   plain facts: the tree search, the canopy and the shell, the fell plan and the replant, the orb rules, the freeze rules,
   the store and the fluid of the Experience Collector, and each adapter's mapping of a mod's machine to its fuel, heat, air,
   torque or growth. For an adapter of another mod, the facts come from the pinned jar, read once with `javap` and `unzip` in
   the task's reference check, as ADR-0015 did for the crops of 1.1. No game starts.
2. **Data tests, unit tests.** The role files, tags and data maps are data. The tests read every entry of another mod as
   optional (CD-OPT-12), every default family in its tag, and every role file of a compat package with its condition
   (CD-OPT-03).
3. **GameTests of the main run, vanilla and Create and AE2 only.** They prove the glue that a unit test cannot: real trees,
   the Experience Collector, real orbs, real water, the Healing Machine, the farmland Pump, the Gilded Chest, the budding
   tumblestone, the AE2 crank, the heat users above a burner, and the claims and leashes of the engine. Each task lists its
   GameTests, about 20 in all.
4. **The test pack's case `mods`.** One dedicated server with the built jar and every mod of the table of ADR-0016 that the
   test pack can fetch from Modrinth or copy from `TESTPACK_LOCAL_JARS`. It reads the compat lines (CD-OPT-11), the roles line
   and the mixins line, so a renamed class, a lost block id or a mixin that did not apply shows in seconds on real jars. If two
   mods cannot load together, the case runs the groups of Task 23 one after the other.
5. **The compat GameTest run.** `runGameTestServerCompat` runs outside CI, at the gate. It holds one GameTest for each adapter
   that writes a mod's private state through a mixin, an accessor or reflection: the IE watermill and windmill, the Mekanism wind
   generator, Easy Magic and Enchanting Infuser, the Liquid Blaze Burner of Create Crafts & Additions, the Egg Incubator of
   Cobblemon Incubator, the Clibano of Forbidden & Arcanus, the compressors of PneumaticCraft and the Alchemist Cauldron of
   Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. The other adapters write public fields or call public methods, so their unit tests and the
   case `mods` cover them, and the COHO mods also get the Demos of checks 63 to 66 on the COHO server, where the approver
   plays.

## Global Constraints

The [Global Constraints of the Phase 2 plan](2026-11-10-phase-2-plan.md#global-constraints), the
[Phase 3 additions](2026-11-11-phase-3-release.md#global-constraints) and the [1.1 additions](2026-11-12-v1.1-farming.md#global-constraints)
hold. 1.2 adds these lines:

- A class that names an optional mod lives only in `compat/<modid>/` or `mixin/compat/<modid>/`, and only a guarded
  `register` loads it (CD-OPT-05, CD-OPT-06).
- A role file of an optional mod holds its `neoforge:mod_loaded` condition (CD-OPT-03).
- Every entry of another mod in a tag or data map is optional (CD-OPT-12): `"required": false` in a tag, and a
  `neoforge:mod_loaded` condition on the entry in a data map. NeoForge applies no condition at the top of either file.
- The new handlers post no level event, play no sound, and call neither `Level.destroyBlock` nor a block's use method. They
  post no break event and use no fake player.
- A handler that changes or breaks a block asks the land claim questions in the tick of the change, for every such block
  (CD-LUM-12, CD-FRZ-10, CD-FRM-07, CD-MCH-09).
- Every new number lives in a role file, except `experienceCollector.capacity`, the one new key of the server config
  (CD-CFG-20).
- 1.2 adds no network payload of its own, so the payload version stays 3. The collector's button uses vanilla's container
  button packet (CD-XPC-05), and its menu type joins the registries.
- Player-facing text calls the creatures Cobblemon.

## How to execute this plan

1. **Before Task 1.** The coordinator rules on the [design decisions](#design-decisions), and the approver answers Q12 to
   Q20 of the spec sheet. Every row of 1.2 is `draft` until then, and nobody builds against a `draft` row.
2. **Branches.** `v12-core` holds Tasks 1 to 9 and Task 22, in one pull request. The compatibility tasks, 10 to 21, each fit
   one branch and one build worker, from `main` after the merge of `v12-core`, because each task reads its own mod's jars.
   Two compat tasks that touch the same handler, such as Tasks 17 and 2 for the Crystal Tender, merge in task order. Task 23
   runs after the compat tasks, and Task 24 on `main` after the last merge.
3. **References.** At the start of each task, check each reference of the task and of [Checked references](#checked-references)
   against the pinned jar. Where the code differs from this plan, fix the plan in the same commit as the code. A reference
   marked "unverified" must be checked before its code is written.
4. **Test first.** Write the failing test, run it and see it fail for the expected reason, write the code, run the test and
   see it pass, then commit.
5. **Done.** A task is done when its tests pass, `./gradlew build` passes, and its GameTests pass in their run. A branch is
   done when its pull request is green in CI, every review thread is resolved, and it is merged.
6. **The machine.** Follow the development notes: JDK 21 through `JAVA_HOME`, one JVM at a time on the shared machine, the JVM
   lock, and one line to COHO-2 before each large JVM run. The case `mods` and the compat run are large runs.
7. **Coverage.** After any plan edit, `py scripts/check_plan_coverage.py` with each of `--phase 1`, `--phase 2`, `--phase 3`,
   `--phase 1.1` and `--phase 1.2` prints `missing: 0`.

## Design decisions

The rulings settle the features. This plan made the calls below. Calls 1 to 9 are Q12 to Q20 of the spec sheet, and calls
10 to 35 are technical. The coordinator ruled on all of them on 2026-11-15 (R81 of the spec sheet), under the approver's
delegation: "go ahead and approve the technical calls pre-emptively. i will trust in your calls". Every default
stands, except calls 1 and 30, which name their rulings. Calls 1 to 9 set how the game plays, so the approver's go on the plan summary confirms them.

1. **The Lumberjack's pace (Q12, approver).** Default: 10 ticks of Working per log at every level, as an iron axe, and Work
   Stamina only through Working. Options: a pace by level, from 20 ticks per log at level 1 to 5 at level 100, or an extra
   cost of Work Stamina per log. **Ruling (R81):** the pace by level, with Work Stamina only through Working.
2. **The Lumberjack's defaults (Q13, approver).** Default: at most 1,024 leaves, mangroves skipped, four saplings or one for a
   2 × 2 trunk with dark oak only as four, the shell felled with the tree, and trees with a bee nest or a beehive skipped.
   Options: fell a mangrove with no replant, require four saplings for every 2 × 2 trunk, or skip every tree with an attached
   block.
3. **The Soul Collector (Q14, approver).** Default: ceil(10 × L ÷ 100) orb units per second, so level 100 takes as a player
   does, shared claims of up to 5 workers on one collector, and Work Stamina only through Working. Options: one worker per
   collector, or an extra cost per orb unit.
4. **The Experience Collector (Q15, approver).** Default: a capacity of 100,000 XP (the key takes 1,000 to 1,000,000) and the
   recipe of 4 iron ingots, 3 glass, a block of lapis lazuli and a hopper. Options: another capacity, or a dearer recipe with
   an Eye of Ender or a Sculk Catalyst.
5. **The Freezer's pace (Q16, approver).** Default: ceil(60 × L ÷ 100) cells per minute, Work Stamina only through Working,
   and no cell at block light 10 or more. Options: a faster curve, or freezing lit water that melts again.
6. **The Heater (Q17, approver).** Default: ceil(1,500 × L ÷ 100) J/t on the Mekanism valves, a virtual lava block on
   PneumaticCraft, and leash only on PneumaticCraft. Options: the Generator's exponential curve on Mekanism, or
   auto-assignment on PneumaticCraft.
7. **The Egg Incubator (Q18, approver).** Default: build it as a Stoker target, capped at one Speed Upgrade. Option: cut it as
   an animal row of R80. It needs Cobbreeding, whose Modrinth license adds two terms to MIT (ADR-0016). **Ruling (R81):**
   the default. Neither term covers an optional integration or a local test-pack download.
8. **When the fixes ship (Q19, approver).** Default: with 1.2.0. Option: a patch 1.1.1 first, with its own gate and tag.
9. **The move effects (Q20, approver).** Default: Aerial Ace (Lumberjack), Night Shade (Soul Collector), Ice Beam (Freezer)
   and Ember (Heater), each checked for silence. Option: another silent effect from RES9's candidates.
10. **The roles of optional mods.** Default: one handler and one role file per mod family, with a `neoforge:mod_loaded`
    condition. Options: adapters inside the existing handlers, or handler gates with no conditions.
11. **Skipped role files.** Default: their own INFO line (CD-OPT-04). Option: a tail on the roles line of CD-CMP-10.
12. **Create's role files.** Default: they keep their handler gate in 1.2, so CD-CFG-10 and the test pack's case without
    Create stay. Option: conditions for them too, which changes those tests.
13. **Quick Work opt-out.** Default: `"quick_work": false` keeps an auto-assign role out of Quick Work, an additive meaning in
    role file schema 1. Option: a flag on the handler.
14. **The collector's button.** Default: vanilla's container button packet, so the payload version stays 3. Option: a payload
    of our own, which makes the version 4 and gives CD-CMP-09 a 1.2 part.
15. **The collector's fluid.** Default: CEI's fluid at 1 mB per XP first, else the first fluid of `c:experience` at 20, with no
    override key, and drains in whole XP. Option: a config key or a tag that overrides the fluid.
16. **experiencelib.** Default: a drain-only XP capability on the outputs, compile-only against experiencelib 1.2.1. Option:
    leave it out, so an ME network takes the XP only as a fluid.
17. **The fell.** Default: every claim question in the first tick, asked once per chunk and block id, then a removal of 128
    blocks per tick that completes at once on an unclaim. Options: a fell in one tick, or the questions in each removal tick.
18. **The canopy.** Default: the pure rule of the tree's own leaves. Option: remove the logs, wait for vanilla's distances, and
    take the leaves at distance 7.
19. **The shell.** Default: `canSurvive` against a view with the tree removed. Option: a tag of attached blocks.
20. **The Freezer's claim.** Default: one water cell per claim, the work within reach of the worker, the claimed cell last,
    and a harmless overlap. Option: one claim per zone of the work area.
21. **The wind generator's hook.** Default: the return of `getMultiplier()`, so Mekanism's screen shows the bonus and the bonus
    lapses within 20 ticks. Option: the return of `getCurrentGeneration()`, which lapses at once but leaves the screen wrong.
22. **The IE Watermill's torque.** Default: measure an optimized overshot wheel in the compat run and set `virtual_torque` to
    it, about 29 (unverified). Option: a fixed 29.
23. **The IE Windmill's sync.** Default: a synced attachment, so the windmill turns on screen at the speed of its power
    (CD-AIR-06 is a SHOULD). Option: no sync.
24. **The Railcraft firebox.** Default: each firebox block is a target, with at most 1.0 burn tick per tick each. Option: one
    target per boiler.
25. **Botany Pots.** Default: two pot handlers in the farming lanes. Option: a pot branch in the crop handlers.
26. **Twilight Forest bushes.** Default: the data map `cobblemon_dynamics:harvest_loot`, because the names of the berry tables
    differ by block. Option: a rule in code.
27. **Mystical Agriculture's crops.** Default: the Cultivator's never tag. Option: a code check of `isBonemealSuccess`
    before a use.
28. **The Crystal Tender's stall.** Default: 200 unchanged ticks stop a target for 6,000 ticks. Option: no stall.
29. **The Container fallback.** Default: every vanilla `Container` with no item capability, except a deny tag (Lootr's
    containers, Legendary Monuments' pedestals, ids unverified). Option: an allow tag per mod.
30. **The Muse at Enchanting Infuser with Apothic Enchanting.** Default: add the bookshelves to the infuser's power, which it
    counts as Eterna. Option: nothing in that mode. **Ruling (R81):** nothing in that mode, because the Muse gives only Quanta
    with Apothic Enchanting (CD-MUS-07).
31. **The compatibility test approach (ruling 6 of R80).** Default: the five layers of [Test approach](#test-approach): unit
    tests per adapter, the data tests, the main GameTest run, one test-pack boot `mods` with the fallback groups, and a compat
    GameTest run outside CI for the adapters that write private state, with the Demos of checks 63 to 66 on COHO. Options:
    no compat GameTests and Demos only, or one boot per mod.
32. **The test pack's downloads.** Default: Modrinth pins by SHA-512, local jars for the Twilight Forest and Ars Elemental,
    Just Dire Things left out, and each license read before its pin. Option: CurseForge downloads, which need an API key.
33. **Compile coordinates.** Default: Modrinth's maven for each compile-only dependency, resolved in Task 1. Option: each
    mod's own maven.
34. **Dynamic Trees' own fell.** Default: accept the sounds that Dynamic Trees may play, as its own rule. Option: skip Dynamic
    Trees trees.
35. **The balance flags.** Default: record and accept the Seething heat of a level 50 Superheater in PneumaticCraft machines
    and the SU of the Generator through Create: New Age's motors (CD-BAL-17). Option: cap them.
